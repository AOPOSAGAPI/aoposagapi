💪 Become a Volunteer

Volunteering is the soul of the NGO. We don't ask for money — we ask for your time and your heart.

🎯 Areas of Volunteering

  • Shelter: dog walks, care, adoptions
  • Schools: accompanying awareness presentations
  • Festivals: support for events & festivals
  • Elderly Centers: activities for older people
  • Administrative: paperwork, social media, translation
  • Sign Language: bridge to the deaf community (if you know GSL)

📋 What does it involve?

There is no minimum commitment time — you can give as much time as your life allows. Activities at the Shelter are coordinated with the City of Athens.
