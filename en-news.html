Saturday 24 October 2026 · Tae Kwon Do Arena, Faliro

We were at the 1st Volunteering Festival of the Attica Region

From 11 am to 5 pm, at our own booth, we talked with dozens of visitors about the three pillars of our work: caring for stray animals, integrating migrants and supporting people with disabilities. We handed out cards, explained our goals — and the chocolate bars were a big hit!

But the star of the day was Minnoş. The most moving moments were his cuddles with fellow citizens with disabilities — a reminder that the bond between people and animals opens hearts without words.

Warm thanks to Christos from the Shelter and to Jan for keeping us company all day, to the Attica Region for the initiative, and to everyone who visited our booth.

Past Activities of the Group

📚 Migrant Sunday School

For many years, Greek lessons for refugees and migrants in junior and senior high school venues across Athens.

👴 Actions in Elderly Centers (KAPI)

Visits to KAPI elderly centers for older people experiencing abandonment and loneliness — a little company makes a difference.

🎢 Children from institutions to amusement parks

Organized trips for children from institutions and orphans to amusement parks — moments of joy and carefree play.

📐 METAdrasi — Mathematics

Volunteer math teaching for migrant children through METAdrasi. There also began contact with the Deaf community.

🤟 Sign Language

Learning Greek Sign Language at "Kratylos" School — the start of a new bridge with the deaf community.

🎭 "Black Board" Theatre & Festival

Participation in a theatre piece with social message and the "Pets in Action" festival under the auspices of the City of Athens.
