Our Journey

From the informal group actions of Sotiris Perakis and the long-standing volunteer work of our members, to the establishment of the NGO and the vision of the Lighthouse of Love.

The model we are building

Every Greek lesson to a refugee, every visit to an elderly center, every walk with a dog, every sign language word learned — is a brick of the Lighthouse of Love being built slowly but surely.

Before 2018 · The Roots

Informal "A for Agapi" group – Sotiris Perakis

The group is born with the vision of supporting vulnerable social groups. Sotiris Perakis coordinates it and captures its work in a book of the same name.

For many years · Migrant Sunday School

Greek lessons for refugees & migrants

Georgios Gkolias teaches Greek as a volunteer in junior and senior high school venues across Athens, through the Migrant Sunday School.

Parallel actions

Elderly centers (KAPI) & children from institutions

Visits to KAPI elderly centers for older people experiencing abandonment and loneliness. Organized trips for children from institutions and orphans to amusement parks — moments of joy for those who need them most.

METAdrasi · Volunteer Teaching

Mathematics for migrant children

Volunteer participation at METAdrasi. There, something else also begins — the first contact with the world of the Deaf.

A new journey · Sign Language

Greek Sign Language proficiency from "Kratylos" School

Georgios begins learning Greek Sign Language — a different, magical world. He gains GSL proficiency. One of his teachers is Christos Dagres, founder of the GSL "Kratylos" School — today our treasurer and a key member of the NGO Board.

2018 · Athens Municipal Shelter

First volunteer presence with stray animals

First presence at the Municipal Stray Animal Shelter of the City of Athens. A long, continuous volunteer relationship begins — and continues to this day.

2019–2024 · Continuous Action

Walks, adoptions, school presentations

Oreo and Zoe — strays from the Shelter — become the "ambassadors" who travel to schools.

2023 · Institutionalization

Founding of the NGO "A for Agapi"

All these parallel paths — Sotiris, migrants, elderly, children, deaf, animals — are embodied in one institutionalized entity. The group of people becomes an NGO.

September 2025 · NGO Initiative

Renovation of iron staircase at the Shelter

Through the initiative of the NGO and the work of Mustafa Megis, migrant and member.

2025–2026 · Awareness

44 school presentations across Athens

From kindergartens to high schools. 26 in 2025, 18 in 2026 (through April).

2026 → · The Vision

The Lighthouse of Love

Launching partnerships with organizations, foundations and municipalities to make the Lighthouse a reality — where all groups will meet.

Board of Directors

Five people with years of giving back, each with their own journey through vulnerable groups

Georgios Gkolias

President & Founder

7+ year volunteer at the Athens Municipal Stray Animal Shelter. Greek lessons for refugees & migrants through the Sunday Migrant School, mathematics through METAdrasi, proficiency in Greek Sign Language. Coordinator of school awareness programs, founder and president of the NGO.

Athanasios Tetoros

Vice President

Co-founder and vice president of the NGO. Supports strategic planning, institutional partnerships, and the evolution of the Lighthouse of Love vision.

Dimitra Lexouritis

Secretary

Manages the administrative operations, communications, and organization of NGO activities. An important bridge between members and partner organizations.

Christos Dagres

Treasurer

Founder of the Greek Sign Language School "Kratylos" and the NGO's living bridge to the deaf community. Manages the NGO's finances with full transparency. Played a decisive role in the founder's new journey into the world of sign language.

🤟 Founder of GSL "Kratylos" School

Lazaros Gkolias

Board Member

Actively contributes to the NGO's actions and initiatives, with continuous support for daily needs and the realization of the vision.
