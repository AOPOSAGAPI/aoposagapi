A Greek non-profit organization acting for stray animals, people with disabilities, and the integration of migrants. We act with love, consistency, and real deeds.

Who We Are

The NGO "A for Love" was founded by people who believe that change starts with actions — not words. We operate in Athens and collaborate with organizations, municipalities, and volunteers across three pillars.

Our founder and president, Georgios Gkolas, has been a volunteer for 7+ years at the Municipal Stray Animal Shelter of the City of Athens. This experience shaped the philosophy and vision of the NGO.

PROGRAM: THE LIGHTHOUSE OF LOVE

Our vision for a model center that will shelter stray animals, provide services to people with disabilities, and serve as an integration hub for migrants. Three worlds meeting under one roof of love.

"Imagine a space where a migrant trains a dog while a child with a disability plays alongside them. That is the Lighthouse of Love."

News & Activities

Our founder participates as a volunteer at the Municipal Shelter — and this experience fuels our vision.

September 2025

Iron Staircase Renovation — NGO Initiative

Through the initiative of the NGO founder and the voluntary work of Mustafa Megis — an NGO member and migrant from Turkey integrating into Greek society — the deteriorated iron staircase at the Shelter was fully renovated. A living proof that integration happens through action.

2025–2026 | 44 Presentations

School Awareness — Oreo Opens Hearts

Georgios Gkolas and Oreo — a hydrocephalic, epileptic dog adopted from the Shelter — visit kindergartens, primary and secondary schools. Children meet stray animals, learn responsible adoption, and leave with drawings and love.

2025–2026 | Institutional Collaboration

Meetings with the Mayor of Athens & the Deputy Mayor for Animal Welfare

Volunteer visit to Athens City Hall to discuss the operating issues of the Shelter. Also, a working meeting at the Shelter with the Deputy Mayor and the President of the Pan-Hellenic Federation "Nemesis" on action planning.

Ongoing Activity

Schools Visit the Shelter

Students and teachers from primary and evening schools visit the Shelter for a hands-on experience. A volunteer team welcomes them, introduces Socrates and the animals — and children leave chanting: "We love strays — We adopt responsibly!"

The Story of Mustafa

From Migrant to Volunteer — Mustafa Megis

Mustafa Megis, a registered NGO member from Turkey, actively participates in our activities. He renovated the Shelter staircase and takes part in festivals. A living example of our vision: the NGO doesn't talk about integration — it practices it.

Ongoing Adoptions

Dozens of Dogs Found a Home

The adoption of two sibling puppies by a foreign family living in Greece. Volunteers actively support the adoption process — every new home is a victory.

Primary School Athens

"We love animals and we will never hurt them"

Primary school children gifted their drawings to the NGO after a presentation. Teachers helped children paint the theme "I Love Animals" — and brought them as a gift to the Shelter visit.
