Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data controller

A OPOS AGAPI Civil Non-Profit Company, 25 Akadimias St, 106 71 Athens, Greece · Tax ID 996560907 · info@aoposagapi.org.

2. What we collect

Only what you give us: your name, email and message in the contact form; your name and email when you subscribe to the newsletter; and the details you enter in the membership and volunteer forms. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

3. Why

To reply to your message, send you our monthly newsletter (only with your consent), manage memberships and organise volunteering. We never sell or share your data for commercial purposes.

4. Service providers

The contact form runs on Formspree; the newsletter and the membership/volunteer forms on Google Forms; the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data only on our behalf.

5. How long we keep it

Only as long as needed: messages until the conversation is complete, newsletter details until you unsubscribe, member details for the duration of membership and as required by law.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data and withdraw consent at any time by writing to info@aoposagapi.org. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter by replying "UNSUBSCRIBE". You may also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

7. Photos

Photos on this website are published with the consent of the people shown. If you appear in a photo and want it removed, let us know.
